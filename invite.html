Calendar invite

Text for the meeting invite. Copy each part into your calendar. Details are subject to change.

Title

🚀 AI Enabled Product Hackathon: idea to live product in a day

When

Wednesday 28 October 2026, 10:00–17:00

Location

LBG office, Westminster House, 11 Portland Street, Manchester, M1 3HU. Event space, 6th floor

Invite description

Got an idea you’ve always wanted to build? This is your day to make it real.

Join us for a hands-on hackathon where you’ll use AI to take a product from first idea to a live website that anyone can open. No coding experience needed. Bring your curiosity and your own idea (or pick one of our prepared briefs). You’ll leave with something you made and the confidence to keep going.

Where to go
LBG office, Westminster House, 11 Portland Street, Manchester, M1 3HU. Head to the event space on the 6th floor. Arrive from 09:30 for coffee and to get settled. We start strictly at 10:00.

📌 Please book a desk
Book a desk for the day so you have somewhere to leave your things, and a quiet spot to work if you need a break from the buzz.

The day at a glance
09:30 Doors open: arrive, connect to Wi-Fi, grab a coffee
10:00 Welcome and kick-off
Morning: talks, publish your first page, shape your idea, start building
12:30 Lunch
Afternoon: build, test with others, improve
16:00 Demos: show what you made (about three minutes, no slides needed)
17:00 Wrap-up
18:00 Drinks at Lane7, Great Northern Warehouse, 235 Deansgate

Bring
A personal Mac or Windows laptop and its charger. No personal laptop? An LBG laptop with Figma Make, or a phone or tablet, also works.

Before the day: a short warm-up (about 30 minutes)
Follow the warm-up at https://hackday.bonhard.ai (password sent separately). The warm-up is optional, but please do it if you possibly can: it will make getting started much easier. Without it, you’ll spend some of the event setting up.

Optional setup clinics
Monday 26 October, 11:00–11:30, and Tuesday 27 October, 10:30–11:00 (UK time). Calendar invitations for these will be emailed to everyone who has accepted this invitation. If in doubt, check with Phil Bonhard.

Please read
This is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites.

Questions? Message Phil Bonhard on LBG Microsoft Teams.

Details are subject to change. Further information will follow closer to the day.
