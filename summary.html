          How this was worked out

          • Participants: people who took part.
          • AI scores: complexity, creativity, breadth, clarity, focus, testability and usefulness, each out of 10, from the PRD only. Ties share a place.
          • People’s choice: each voter picked up to three favourites on the vote page, one set of votes per device, on trust.
          • What we show: first names and project names only. PRDs are never published. Asked not to be named, or left your project out of the vote? It appears without a name.
