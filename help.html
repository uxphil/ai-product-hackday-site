Ask Phil

Phil Bonhard is organising the day. Find Phil on LBG Microsoft Teams. Say which step you’re on and what you can see.

Setup clinic To be confirmed

A 60-minute drop-in is proposed for Thursday 22 October 2026. Time and place (or video link) aren’t confirmed and nobody has been invited yet.

On the day

Helpers To be confirmed will be around the room, with a visible way to ask for one. They’ll ask you to operate the tool while they guide, because it’s your product.

The clinic is optional and for anyone who’s stuck, who’d like company while doing the warm-up, or who isn’t sure their AI plan will cope with a full day. Bring your laptop and whatever stage you’ve reached. It’s fine to arrive having done nothing.

When to ask

  • If you’ve been stuck for about ten minutes, ask.

  • If a screen asks for payment or a card, stop and ask.

  • If you’re unsure whether something is safe to use, ask first.

Before you ask, try this

Page won’t publish or shows 404

See the notes under steps 7 and 8 of the GitHub route.

My AI assistant says I’ve run out of usage

Stop, and don’t buy anything on the spot. Use the ready-made page in step 1 of the warm-up and tell Phil. We’d rather help you plan around limits than have you pay unexpectedly.

I can’t sign up for something

Skip it, use a different route, and ask at the clinic. Don’t use a work account to get round a problem.
