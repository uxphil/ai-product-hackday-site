How long will it take?

These are our estimates and haven’t yet been tested with first-time users. Most people should need about 30–40 minutes in total, or 20–30 if you already have a GitHub account. You can do it in two sittings: Part 1 needs no accounts.

StepEstimateNote
Part 1 · Make the page on your computer8–12 minNo accounts needed
Create a GitHub account and verify your email5–10 minSkip if you already have one
Create a repository and upload the page4–6 min
Turn on publishing2–3 min
Wait for the first publish1–10 minGitHub says up to 10 minutes. Read ahead while you wait
Open it on your phone1–2 min
Part 3 · Change one sentence and publish again4–12 minIncludes another wait

Signing up for your AI assistant isn’t counted, as you’ll already have chosen and paid for it.

What you don’t need

No product brief, framework, database, API key, custom domain, payment setup or special plug-in. No terminal and no installing coding tools. The real product work begins on the day.

Stuck?

If something stops you for about ten minutes, ask for help. There’s a setup clinic for exactly this To be confirmed.
