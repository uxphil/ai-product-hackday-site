Get ready

What you need to know, what to bring, and a short warm-up to do beforehand. Your ticks are saved on this device only.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

Why tools outside work? Explore what is possible with AI tools outside work, and build skills you can carry into your design practice. The tools you try on the day may not be available on your work machine. What travels with you is how you frame a problem, question assumptions, direct AI, test an idea and improve it. More about this.

The essentials

  • When. Wednesday 28 October 2026. Arrive from 09:30 for coffee and to get settled. We start strictly at 10:00 and finish at 17:00.

  • Where. LBG office, Westminster House, 11 Portland Street, Manchester, M1 3HU. Event space, 6th floor. Please book a desk for the day.

  • Bring. Your own Mac or Windows laptop and its charger. No personal laptop? Bring your LBG laptop (with Figma Make) or a phone or tablet: see how you’ll take part.

Before the day: get set up

These steps are for a personal laptop. On an LBG laptop or a phone, follow your route’s page instead.

The warm-up is optional, but please do it if you possibly can: it will make getting started much easier. Without it, you’ll spend some of the event setting up. Not managed it? You can still come. Tell Phil or a helper when you arrive, and expect to spend some of the event getting set up.

  1. Read the safety note1 min

    It’s the box above and takes a minute. Optional detail.

  2. Download your AI tool and sign in5–10 min

    Install the Claude desktop app (needs a paid Claude plan) or the ChatGPT desktop app with Codex (works on a free account, within usage limits), on your own laptop with your own account. We recommend Claude, but buying a plan is your choice: Codex is the free route. Not sure which to pick? The warm-up page helps you choose. Unsure you have access or enough usage? Bring it to a setup clinic.

    Choose your tool

  3. Create a GitHub account5–10 min

    Free and personal. We’ll use it on the day to put your page online, so please have it ready. Go to github.com/signup, use your own email and verify it. Already have one? Tick this off.

  4. Do the hello-world warm-upabout 15 min

    Ask your AI tool to make a web page that says hello world. It may ask you a question or two, or just make the page: either is fine. Then look at the page on your own computer. Nothing is published. This ticks itself when you finish.

    Start the warm-up

  5. Look at the project themes3 min

    Pick one that appeals, or bring your own idea. You only choose a theme: don’t start building it.

    See the themes

  6. Charge up and pack

    Laptop, charger, and your hello-world folder on the laptop (if you did the warm-up).

Optional extra: add a smiley to your page and, if you like, publish it a day early. Open the optional steps. You don’t need them: on the day we’ll publish together.

You’re ready

Your setup is done, so you can get going straight away on the day. See you on Wednesday 28 October: arrive from 09:30, and we start at 10:00 sharp. Where to go and what to bring are in the essentials above.

How long will it take?

These are our estimates and haven’t yet been tested with first-time users. Most people should need about 20–30 minutes in total, or 15–20 if you already have a GitHub account. You can do it in two sittings: install the tool and make the account first.

StepEstimateNote
Download your AI tool and sign in5–10 minClaude or ChatGPT desktop app
Create a GitHub account and verify your email5–10 minSkip if you already have one
Make an empty folder2 min
Ask for the page (and answer any questions)5–10 minDepends how chatty the tool is
Open the page on your computer1–2 min
Optional · Add a smiley3–5 minNot needed to be ready
Super optional · Publish with GitHub15–30 minIncludes a wait of up to 10 minutes. We do this together on the day

Buying a paid AI plan isn’t counted: that’s your own choice, and Codex works without one.

What you don’t need

No product brief, framework, database, API key, custom domain, payment setup or special plug-in. No terminal. The only install is your AI tool’s desktop app. The real product work begins on the day.

Stuck?

If something stops you for about ten minutes, ask for help, or come to one of the optional setup clinics on Monday 26 or Tuesday 27 October.
