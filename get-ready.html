Get ready

A tool, an account, one tiny page and a few small things. Your ticks are saved on this device only.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

  1. Read the safety note1 min

    It’s the box above and takes a minute. Optional detail.

  2. Download your AI tool and sign in5–10 min

    Install the Claude desktop app (paid Claude plan) or the ChatGPT desktop app with Codex (free or paid), on your own laptop with your own account. The steps are on the warm-up page below. If you’re not sure you have access or usage left, mention it at the clinic.

  3. Create a GitHub account5–10 min

    Free and personal. We’ll use it on the day to put your page online, so please have it ready. Go to github.com/signup, use your own email and verify it. Already have one? Tick this off.

  4. Do the hello-world warm-upabout 15 min

    Ask your AI tool to make a web page that says hello world. It will ask you some questions: answer them. Then look at the page on your own computer. Nothing is published. This ticks itself when you finish.

    Start the warm-up

  5. Look at the project themes3 min

    Pick one that appeals, or bring your own idea. You only choose a theme: don’t start building it.

    See the themes

  6. Charge up and pack

    Laptop, charger, and your hello-world folder on the laptop.

Optional extra: add a smiley to your page and, if you like, publish it a day early. Open the optional steps. You don’t need them: on the day we’ll publish together.

How long will it take?

These are our estimates and haven’t yet been tested with first-time users. Most people should need about 20–30 minutes in total, or 15–20 if you already have a GitHub account. You can do it in two sittings: install the tool and make the account first.

StepEstimateNote
Download your AI tool and sign in5–10 minClaude or ChatGPT desktop app
Create a GitHub account and verify your email5–10 minSkip if you already have one
Make an empty folder2 min
Ask for the page and answer its questions5–10 minDepends how chatty the tool is
Open the page on your computer1–2 min
Optional · Add a smiley3–5 minNot needed to be ready
Super optional · Publish with GitHub15–30 minIncludes a wait of up to 10 minutes. We do this together on the day

Signing up for your AI tool’s paid plan isn’t counted, as you’ll already have chosen it.

What you don’t need

No product brief, framework, database, API key, custom domain, payment setup or special plug-in. No terminal. The only install is your AI tool’s desktop app. The real product work begins on the day.

Stuck?

If something stops you for about ten minutes, ask for help. There’s a setup clinic for exactly this To be confirmed.
