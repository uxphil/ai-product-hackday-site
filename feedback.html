How was the day?

Eight quick questions, about three minutes. What you tell us shapes the next hackathon.

Anonymous unless you choose to leave your email. Questions 1 to 4 need an answer; the rest are optional.

1.Overall, how would you rate the event?

2.How well would you rate the following aspects of the day?

Explaining the purpose of the day

Presenting concepts clearly

Keeping your interest

Encouraging participation

Answering questions

3.How likely are you to recommend this event to a colleague?

0 · Not at all likely10 · Extremely likely

4.How did the event compare with your expectations?

Only Phil sees it, only to follow up on your feedback. Never shared, and deleted 30 days after the event.

What happens to it

  • Your answers go to Phil Bonhard, the organiser, to improve the next event. They’re stored against a random code saved in this browser. No names, no sign-in.
  • Your answers are anonymous. If you leave your email, we use it only to follow up on your feedback, never share it, and delete it 30 days after the event.
  • Phil may quote a comment in a write-up of the day, without saying who wrote it.
  • Changed your mind? Send it again from this device and it replaces your earlier answers.
