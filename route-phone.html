Get ready · Phone or tablet route

Build it on your phone

No laptop? You can do the whole day on a phone or tablet. Typing is harder, but it’s a fun way to find out how little you need a computer to plan, build and publish something. The warm-up takes about 15 minutes (an estimate).

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

Use your own phone or tablet and your own personal accounts, not a work phone.

Choose your tool

ClaudeRecommended · free or paid plan

The Claude app

On iPhone, iPad or Android. You plan and build in a chat. Claude makes your page as an artifact (a live preview inside the chat) and you publish it as a link. Works on the free plan; a paid plan gives you more room.

Use Claude
LovableAlternative · free plan with daily credits

Lovable in your browser

Open lovable.dev in Safari or Chrome. It builds and publishes a web app, and can save it to GitHub. The free plan has a small number of build credits a day.

Use Lovable

The Lovable warm-up is written for a laptop, but it works the same in your phone’s browser.

Warm-up with Claude

These steps follow Claude’s documentation and haven’t yet been tried by us on a phone. Button names may differ slightly between iPhone, iPad and Android.

  1. Read the safety note1 min

    It’s the box at the top of this page.

  2. Install the Claude app and sign in3–5 min

    Get it from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in or sign up with your personal email.

  3. Ask for the page5 min

    Start a new chat and paste this in. Open this site on your phone so you can use the Copy button.

    Please make a single-page website as an artifact, with a heading that says "Hello, world", one friendly sentence, and a button that changes the sentence when tapped. Use sample text only. Make it look good on a phone.

    Tap the preview to open it full screen, and try the button.

  4. Change it with your voice2 min

    Tap the microphone on your keyboard and say: “Change the sentence to something about the weather.” Speaking is much quicker than typing on a phone, so it’s worth practising now.

  5. Publish it3 min

    With the artifact open, look for Publish or Share and copy the link. Can’t find it in the app? Open claude.ai in your phone’s browser, sign in, open the same chat and publish from there.

  6. Check someone else can open it2 min

    Send the link to a friend, or open it on another device. That proves other people can open your work.

You’re ready

  • You built a page on your phone.
  • You changed it by voice.
  • It’s published at a link someone else can open.

Bring your phone or tablet, its charger and a power bank on the day. You’ll start a new chat for your real project.

Saved on this device only

Typing less on a small screen

  • Talk instead of type. Use the microphone on your keyboard for long answers. Claude copes well with rambling.

  • Let the AI ask. Say “ask me one question at a time” so your answers can be short.

  • Copy, don’t retype. Open this site on your phone and use the Copy buttons on the prompts.

  • Power up. Building all day drains a battery. Bring a charger and a power bank. A Bluetooth keyboard helps, but isn’t needed.

On the day: plan, build and publish in one app

You follow the same Plan your product exercise as everyone else. Skip its GitHub steps: they’re for laptops.

  1. Question your ideaabout 15 min

    Start a new chat and paste in the prompt from Plan your product. On a paid plan, create a project first so your PRD and chats stay together.

  2. Keep your PRD as Markdown2 min

    Once you’ve agreed it, ask: “Put the agreed PRD in a Markdown document.” Claude shows it as a document you can open and copy. Keep a copy as a file: save it as PRD.md in your Files app, so you can upload it when you share your PRD for the demos.

  3. Build in small slicesthe rest of the day

    In the same chat, ask Claude to build the first slice from the PRD as a single-page artifact. Check it on your phone, then ask for the next slice.

  4. Publish, and publish again2 min each time

    Publish the artifact and share the link. After changes, publish again and open the link to check it shows the latest version.

Stretch goal: Claude Code on your phone

With a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher), the Code tab in the Claude app can work in a GitHub repository in the cloud. Your code and PRD.md then live on GitHub, like the laptop route. You’ll need a free GitHub account. Ask a helper if you want to try it.

The finish line on this route

Everyone works towards the same six things. Here’s how they look for you.

  • A live address. Your published artifact link (or your Lovable address), opened on someone else’s device.

  • Your reviewed PRD. In your Claude chat or project, with a copy in Notes or Files. It only goes on GitHub if you use Lovable or the stretch goal.

  • Working in a pair? Build in one person’s chat and pass the phone between you. Each make at least one change, and both keep a copy of the PRD.

  • Your demo. Open the live link on your phone and pass it round your group.
