Share your PRD

Send us your PRD and your app’s address before the demos. It takes two minutes, and it lets us put your product on the big screen at the end of the day.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

As you’d like it on screen. Working in a pair? Put both names.

The link someone else can open, starting with https://. Not live yet? Leave it blank and send it again later.

Paste the whole document. In Claude or ChatGPT, use the copy button under the PRD. In Word or Notepad, select all (Ctrl+A or ⌘A) and copy. On GitHub, open PRD.md and use the copy button.

Or share a link instead (GitHub or another public page)

It must open without signing in. Links to OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams, Claude or ChatGPT won’t work: paste the text instead.

What happens to it

  • Your name, project name, app address and PRD go to Phil Bonhard, the organiser. They’re not published on this site.
  • We use them to pick and introduce demos today. Your name and project name may appear on the big screen.
  • Each PRD gets a light-hearted score from an AI tool (Claude), just for fun. It isn’t how anything is judged.
  • Changed something? Send it again from this device and it replaces your earlier one.
  • We delete everything after the event.
