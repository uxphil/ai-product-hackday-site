Share your PRD

Send us your PRD, your app and your product page before the demos. It takes two minutes, and it lets us put your product on the big screen at the end of the day.

Your PRD

Upload the file, or link to it on GitHub. One is enough.

or

Open PRD.md in your repository and copy the address from your browser. The repository must be public.

What happens to it

  • Your name, project name, both addresses and PRD go to Phil Bonhard, the organiser. Your PRD is never published.
  • We use them to pick and introduce demos today. Your name and project name may appear on the big screen.
  • Each PRD gets a light-hearted score from an AI tool (Claude), just for fun. It isn’t how anything is judged.
  • Changed something? Send it again from this device and it replaces your earlier one.
  • After the event, your name, project name and both links appear on the vote page, behind the site password, so other participants can pick their favourites. Untick the box above to leave your project out.
  • We delete everything once voting closes.
