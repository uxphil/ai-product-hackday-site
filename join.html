Join the day

Get your own board for the day: five stops from warm-up to a live app, and a call button for when you’re stuck. It takes a minute.

So a helper knows who to look for.

What your board calls you. Have fun with it.

Roughly is fine, like “Product manager” or “Service designer”.

What happens to it

  • Your name, nickname and job title go to Phil Bonhard, the organiser, with the stops you tick and any call for help. Helpers see them during the day so they can find you.
  • Your board has its own private link with a code in it. Anyone with the link can see and change your board, so keep it to yourself.
  • Opening your board on the day counts you as here. That’s how we count who came.
  • “Forget me” at the bottom of your board deletes it straight away.
  • We delete names, nicknames, job titles and notes 30 days after the event. We keep only anonymous counts, such as how many people reached each stop.
