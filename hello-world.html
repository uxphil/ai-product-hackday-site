Hello-world warm-up

One small page with a heading, a sentence and a button. Publish it, change it, publish again. It’s a rehearsal, not your hackathon product.

  1. Make a page

    About 10 minutes. No accounts needed.

  2. Save to GitHub

    Free personal account, public repository.

  3. Publish it

    An address anyone can open.

  4. Change and republish

    Prove you can iterate.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

See the notes

  • Costs. Every route here can be completed for £0 on a free plan. Free plans change, so if a screen asks for a card or offers a trial, stop and choose the GitHub route.

  • Public by design. The page you publish can be opened by anyone with the address. Use sample text only.

  • Your accounts. Use your own personal accounts, not a work account or work email.

  • Safety. Never put a password, key or access token into a prompt or a page.
