Hello-world warm-up

You’ll make one small page with a heading, a sentence and a button, publish it, change it and publish again. It’s a rehearsal, so it won’t be your hackathon product.

Good to know before you start

  • Costs: every route here can be completed for £0 on a free plan. Free plans change, so if any screen asks for a card or offers a trial, stop and choose the GitHub route instead.
  • Public by design: the page you publish can be opened by anyone with the address. Use sample text only.
  • Your accounts: use your own personal accounts, not a work account or work email.
  • Safety: never put a password, key or access token into a prompt or a page.
