Theme: mortgage journey at a fictional bank

A guided journey that helps a first-time buyer know what’s coming.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

Your actual project starts on the day. This page is a short brief to help you choose a theme. Please don’t start designing or building beforehand. On the day you’ll plan it with AI first.

The problem we’re solving

Getting a first mortgage is confusing. People don’t know the steps, the jargon is unfamiliar, and it’s unclear which documents to gather. Many feel anxious about the first conversation. They want to arrive knowing what to expect and what to bring.

Who it’s for

  • A made-up first-time buyer. Invent a persona with a name and a situation, all fictional, and keep them in view while you design.
  • This is a learning demonstration. It is not a real banking service and does not give financial advice.

Product considerations

Suggestions to think about, not requirements. Use the ones that help.

  • Clearly fictional. Invent a bank name that doesn’t resemble a real one, and don’t use real logos, brands or anything from your employer. Show a “fictional bank, sample data” label on every screen.
  • One step at a time. A short guided flow with visible progress feels calmer than one long form.
  • Plain language. Explain each term in a line the first time it appears.
  • Easy to go back. Let people correct an answer and move backwards without losing the rest.
  • A useful ending. Finish with a coherent summary: the steps ahead and a document checklist they could actually use.
  • Reassuring tone. Say what happens next. Avoid pressure and avoid anything that sounds like a recommendation.
  • No calculations at first. Affordability figures can look authoritative even when made up, so leave them for a stretch idea with stated assumptions.
  • Accessible forms. Clear labels, helpful error messages and large tap targets.
  • Nothing real. Use labelled sample inputs. Nothing is submitted or stored.

First version, in scope

  • A clearly fictional bank.
  • A short guided journey with sample inputs.
  • A next-steps summary or document checklist at the end.
  • Labelled illustrative products and data. No real application is submitted.

How you’ll know it works

  • You can complete the journey from start to finish.
  • You can correct an earlier input.
  • You can move backwards.
  • You reach a coherent summary.
  • The fictional status is visible throughout.

If you finish early, stretch ideas

  • Compare a few illustrative products.
  • A demonstration calculator with the assumptions stated openly.
  • Save sample progress in the browser.
  • An AI explanation feature, using an API (an advanced route, so read the advanced section first).

What to avoid

  • Real bank names, logos or branding, or any LBG material.
  • Real rates or products presented as if they were real.
  • Asking for real personal or financial details.
  • Anything that looks like a real application, or that gives financial advice.
  • Financial calculations in the first version.
