What to bring

Your own Mac or Windows laptop (tablets aren’t suitable) and charger. Your AI tool signed in, your GitHub account ready, and your hello-world page on the laptop. Your first exercise is to put that page on GitHub and publish it.

Working in pairs

Welcome, as long as both people take part. Swap who is at the keyboard about every 25–30 minutes, and each make at least one change. Do the warm-up on your own so you both keep access.

Lunch and refreshments

Lunch is a protected 45 minutes. Catering arrangements: To be confirmed. We’ll say where to share dietary needs once catering is confirmed.
