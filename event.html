Book a desk

Please book a desk in the office for the day. You’ll have somewhere to leave your things, and a quiet spot to work if you need a break from the room.

What to bring

Essential: your own Mac or Windows laptop and charger. No personal laptop? Bring your LBG laptop with Figma Make or a phone or tablet, plus a charger.

Strongly recommended: your AI tool signed in, a GitHub account and your hello-world page from the warm-up. Haven’t done it? Tell Phil or a helper when you arrive, and expect to spend some of the event getting set up.

Working in pairs

Welcome, as long as both people take part. Swap who is at the keyboard about every 25–30 minutes, and each make at least one change. Do the warm-up on your own so you both keep access.

Lunch and refreshments

Lunch is a protected 45 minutes. Catering arrangements: To be confirmed. We’ll say where to share dietary needs once catering is confirmed.

The finish line

Six things to have by the end of the day. Check them at 15:30, before the demos. Your ticks are saved on this device only.

On an LBG laptop or a phone? Your route page shows how each item works for you: LBG laptop, phone or tablet.

What doesn’t count. A screenshot, a preview on your own computer or a link to a builder’s editor isn’t a published product: other people need to be able to open the live address. If publishing fails, ask for help. If it still isn’t live by the end, say so honestly in your demo and write down what’s left to fix. That’s a useful result too.

Your demo

About three minutes, in a small group. No slides needed: show the real thing.

  1. Who it’s for and the problem it solves.
  2. The live product, opened at its address.
  3. What you did yourself.
  4. One thing you learned.
  5. The next improvement you’d make.

Working in a pair? Share the time, and each explain your own contribution.
