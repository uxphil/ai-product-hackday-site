Date

Wednesday 28 October 2026

Time

10:00–17:00, including lunch and breaks

City

Manchester

  • Venue To be confirmed: address, travel notes, Wi-Fi details and a named contact on site.

Agenda

The proposed timetable. Times may shift a little once the venue is confirmed. Helpers arrive at 09:15 and you’re welcome from about 09:30 To be confirmed to settle in and check your Wi-Fi.

  1. 10:00

    Welcome

    Purpose, what counts as “shipped”, and why we’re exploring tools outside work

  2. 10:10

    SaidStore: a real journey

    A live demo, an iteration, and a lesson from getting stuck

  3. 10:25

    How design works in another organisation To be confirmed

    Speaker and content to be confirmed

  4. 10:40

    Publish your hello-world page To be confirmed

    Put your warm-up page on GitHub and make it live

  5. 10:55

    Build briefing and choosing a brief

    One user, one outcome, one small first release. See how questions sharpen an idea

  6. 11:05

    AI interview and your product brief

    Question the idea, agree the journey and checks, then save your PRD. How to plan

  7. 11:30

    Break

  8. 11:40

    Build sprint 1

    Aim for a first version online by 12:30

  9. 12:30

    Lunch

    A protected break. No work required

  10. 13:15

    Build sprint 2

    Core experience working online

  11. 14:15

    Peer testing

    Someone else tries your product; note one finding

  12. 14:35

    Break

  13. 14:45

    Improve, simplify and republish

    No new features after 15:15

  14. 15:30

    Shipping and handover check

    Check the address on another device; save your work

  15. 15:50

    Demo groups

    Everyone shows their product, about three minutes each

  16. 16:25

    Highlights and recognition

  17. 16:50

    Reflection and what next

    The habits that travel with you: framing problems, directing AI, checking results and improving

What to bring

Your own Mac or Windows laptop (tablets aren’t suitable) and charger. Your AI tool signed in, your GitHub account ready, and your hello-world page on the laptop. Your first exercise is to put that page on GitHub and publish it.

Working in pairs

Welcome, as long as both people take part. Swap who is at the keyboard about every 25–30 minutes, and each make at least one change. Do the warm-up on your own so you both keep access.

Lunch and refreshments

Lunch is a protected 45 minutes. Catering arrangements: To be confirmed. We’ll say where to share dietary needs once catering is confirmed.
