What to bring

  • Your own Mac or Windows laptop (tablets aren’t suitable), plus charger.
  • Your AI assistant account, signed in and ready.
  • Your hello-world web address on your phone, to show it works.
  • Headphones are optional, but handy if you like to concentrate.

Working in pairs

Pairs are welcome. Both people must take part: swap who is at the keyboard about every 25–30 minutes, and each of you should make at least one change and check it. Each person should do the hello-world warm-up on their own, so you both keep access to your work.

Lunch and refreshments

Lunch is a protected 45-minute break. Catering arrangements: To be confirmed. If you have dietary needs, we’ll tell you where to say so once catering is confirmed.
