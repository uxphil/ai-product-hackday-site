Project themes

Pick a theme that appeals, or bring your own idea. Your actual project starts on the day. Please don’t begin designing or building it beforehand.

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

Three prepared themes, plus your own

Personal website

For: a visitor who wants to know who you are and how to contact you.

First version: one responsive page with an introduction, two or three sections and a working contact link.

Only include things you’re comfortable having public.

Festive activity planner

For: a busy parent planning the run-up to Christmas.

First version: a small set of sample activities, a way to choose them for dates, and a printable seven-day plan.

No real family or child details.

Mortgage journey, fictional bank

For: a made-up first-time buyer preparing for a mortgage conversation.

First version: a short guided journey with sample inputs and a useful next-steps summary.

Clearly fictional bank, labelled sample data, nothing real is submitted.

Bring your own idea

Choose one user, one job and no more than three essential capabilities.

It has to be something you can publish for free as a website, so helpers may suggest trimming anything that needs real accounts, payments or private data.

What every theme has in common

  • It can be a simple, static website. AI features are optional.

  • It uses fictional or public sample content only.

  • It’s published at an address someone else can open.

  • It’s small enough to finish in one day, with ideas for what next.
