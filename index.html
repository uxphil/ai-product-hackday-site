How the day works

Seven moves from a rough idea to something people can use. Your product doesn’t need AI features: AI is the helper that does the building while you steer.

  1. Converse

    Talk your idea through with AI

  2. Define

    Agree a small first version

  3. Build

    Make it, with helpers around

  4. Publish

    Get it live early

  5. Test

    Someone else tries it

  6. Improve

    Fix the one thing that matters

  7. Continue

    Take it home and keep going

Who it’s for

Experience designers and colleagues

From neighbouring teams too. You don’t need to code. Confidence with AI can be anywhere from “I use it to rewrite emails” to “I’ve built things”.

You’ll leave with

A live web address anyone can open, a short product brief saved in GitHub, and the know-how to change it and publish again.

It costs nothing extra

You bring your own AI subscription. Everything else we use is free, and you never have to pay for anything on the day.

Before the day: one small warm-up

Make a tiny page, publish it, change it, publish again. About 30 minutes, no technical background, no cost. Your real project starts on the day, so please don’t begin it early.

  1. Make a page

    About 10 minutes. No accounts needed.

  2. Save to GitHub

    Free personal account, public repository.

  3. Publish it

    An address anyone can open.

  4. Change and republish

    Prove you can iterate.
