Wednesday 28 October 2026 · Manchester · 10:00–17:00

Build something real. Publish it. Take it home.

A hands-on day where you use AI to turn a product idea, grounded in good design thinking, into a live website you can keep improving.

Get ready (about 30 minutes)

Please read this firstThis is a personal learning exercise. Use fictional or public sample content only. Do not enter, upload or share any LBG data in AI tools, GitHub repositories, prompts, screenshots or published websites. More on staying safe.

What this is

About 50–60 people spend one day designing and building a small product with an AI assistant of their choice. Everyone builds, on their own laptop, alone or in a pair. Your product doesn’t need any AI features: AI is the helper that does the building while you steer the vision.

Who it’s for

Experience designers and colleagues from neighbouring teams. You don’t need to be able to code. Confidence with AI and technology can be anywhere from “I use it to rewrite emails” to “I’ve built things”.

You’ll leave with

  • A live web address that anyone can open
  • A short product brief (PRD) saved in GitHub
  • The know-how to change it and publish again

What it costs

Nothing extra. You bring your own AI subscription and everything else we use is free. You never have to pay for anything on the day.

How the day works

  1. Chat with AI about your idea and agree a small first version.
  2. Build it, with helpers around the room.
  3. Publish it early, then ask someone else to try it.
  4. Improve it, then show it to a small group.

See the agenda and practical details

Before the day

One short warm-up: make a tiny web page, publish it, change it and publish again. It takes about 30 minutes, needs no technical background and costs nothing. Your real project starts on the day, so please don’t begin it early.
